The Lodge GitHub

Who you know, and how well

The Lodge keeps your people in one place: how well you know each of them, how they're connected, and everything you've learned along the way. Drop in notes, meetings and spreadsheets and it files them under the right people. It all lives on your Mac.

Download for Mac
Free and open source · Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 14 or later

Install in three steps

A couple of minutes. Nothing else to install.

Open the download and drag The Lodge to Applications

Double-click TheLodge-arm64.dmg in your Downloads, then drag The Lodge onto the Applications folder next to it.

Open it the first time with “Open Anyway”

Open The Lodge from Applications. macOS says it “can't verify” The Lodge and offers only Done or Move to Trash: click Done. Then:

  1. Open System Settings (Apple menu) → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to “The Lodge” was blocked to protect your Mac and click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click Open Anyway once more.

Why: The Lodge isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Updates install from inside the app without this step.

Bring your people in

A window opens and a small A-frame appears in your menu bar. Add people by hand, or import a spreadsheet or your LinkedIn connections. For the AI parts (reading notes, research, lists), add a Claude or Gemini key in Settings.

What it does

A relationship CRM for one person: the people you know, and what you know about them.

How well, not just who

Rate how close you are to each person. Their profile keeps what you know, where you met and every note, in order.

See your network

A map of your people, gathered around the groups they belong to. Ask who could introduce you to someone, and see the path.

Notes that file themselves

Paste notes or drop in documents. The AI picks out the people, organizations and facts, and puts them where they belong. You review anything it isn't sure of.

Meetings come to you

Connect Granola or a calendar and new meetings arrive on their own, matched to the people who were in them.

Research someone

Flag someone you'd like to know better, and The Lodge researches them with Gemini and Google Search, then files the brief on their profile.

One of everyone

It spots people who are probably the same person and says why. Merges can always be undone.

Lists on request

Ask a focused question, like “who do I know in climate tech?”, and get a list written from your own book.

A sheet others can add to

Share a Google Sheet so people without the app can read, correct and add to your people.

Want the details: how it's built, where data lives, how to run it as a server? It's all on GitHub.

Questions

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 or later. The AI features need your own Claude or Gemini API key; everything else works without one.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Your people and notes are stored on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/The Lodge, readable only by you. Something leaves only when you ask for it: text you have the AI read goes to the AI provider you chose, research sends a person's name and details to Gemini, and a shared Google Sheet holds what you share in it.

How do updates work?

The Lodge checks for new versions and offers to install them from its menu bar or Settings → Updates. Turn on Beta updates there to get new versions a few days early. If one lets you down, Go back returns to the version you had. Your data is backed up on your Mac once a day and kept for two weeks, and you can make a backup any time in Settings → Your data.

Windows, Linux or an Intel Mac?

Not as an app. The server also runs in Docker on Linux; ARCHITECTURE.md on GitHub says how.
